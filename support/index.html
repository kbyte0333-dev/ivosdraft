Help & Support

Resources, Guides, & Issue Reporting

01. Getting Started

Installing or testing InvictusOS is designed to be straightforward. Flash our official system ISO image onto a bootable USB drive using standard tools like Rufus or BalenaEtcher, insert it into your target system, and boot into the live environment.

02. Hardware Requirements

InvictusOS is lightweight by design, allowing older hardware to run fast and smooth without background bloat.

Minimum RAM 512 MB
Recommended RAM 2 GB or higher
Disk Space 8 GB minimum
Architecture x86_64 / ARM64

03. Community Forum

Need advice or custom configuration tips? Join our active open community forum and Discord group. Contributors and experienced users share hardware profiles, kernel tweaks, and custom desktop setups daily.

04. Report an Issue

If you encounter a software bug or broken driver compatibility, please submit a detailed ticket on our open GitHub repository. Include your system hardware specs and boot log to help our development team resolve it quickly.

05. Troubleshooting Common Issues

If your system fails to boot, verify that "Secure Boot" is temporarily disabled in your motherboard's BIOS settings and that your boot drive is formatted with a compatible partition scheme (GPT or MBR).

06. Direct Support

For security reports or developer partnership queries, send a direct note to support@invictusos.org. Our volunteer maintenance team responds within 24 to 48 hours.
