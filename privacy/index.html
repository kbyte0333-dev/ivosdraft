Privacy Policy

Core System Standards & Data Commitments

01. Architecture

We believe that systems should work for you without spying on you. While developing InvictusOS, we have not added any trackers. InvictusOS itself collects zero usage telemetry, zero activity logs, and zero diagnostic reporting. We are not responsible for any data collected by third - party software or data collected by Linux or Debian kernel on which the InvictusOS system is built.

02. Local Storage

Your personal data does not leave your computer. Accounts, configurations, and saved files remain entirely offline on your local storage drive unless you explicitly upload or sync them using third-party software you choose to install or use.

Telemetry & Tracking Disabled
User Accounts Stored offline locally
Crash Diagnostics Never sent automatically

03. Open Source Verification

You don't have to take our word for it. InvictusOS is developed fully in the open under public open-source software licenses. Anyone can inspect, compile, or audit our source code to verify that no hidden backdoors or tracking tools exist.

Please note that InvictusOS is currently still under development. So we cannot absolutely ensure that the code is provided open - source currently. We will surely provide it soon after a while

04. Website & Cookies

This website is just a platform to showcase our OS, features, and provide latest releases to our users. We do not use advertising scripts, analytics tools, or marketing cookies. We do not track your browsing history across the web or sell your information to advertisers.Any data collected or processed outside of our wesite through links redirected from our page is not our responsibility. While you are browsing the other site, you are subject to the privacy policy and T&C of the other site.

Since this OS is being developed by a bunch of teens and we lack large funding, we have hosted the current site on Neocities which is a free platform. We do not hold any responsibility for any data collected by Neocities

05. Your Data Rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on external servers, you retain 100% control over your information at all times. You can modify, export, or permanently delete your local system files and operating system data whenever you wish simply by managing your device drive.

06. Contact Us

If you have any questions, bug reports, or safety inquiries regarding InvictusOS, you can reach out directly to us at osinvictus121@gmail.com or visit our FAQ page. You can also contact us on Discord and we will try our best to answer as fast and be as helpful as possible
